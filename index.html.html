The Autopsy Report for un:fltrd

Every mismatched foundation leaves evidence: the grey cast, the jawline, the “close enough”. The Autopsy Report turns that into a joyful forensic file for your skin, built around the almost ready™ Cushion Foundation. Every finding is a compliment, and the last lines never change: cause of death, compromise. Cause of life, just like: you.

Alive
Subject

Your skin, exactly as it is

Cause of death

Compromise: the “close enough” shade, retired for good

Findings

Undertone, depth and real texture, noted without a single judgement

Cause of life

almost ready™ Cushion Foundation, refined with feedback from 150+ women at the Mumbai shade-matching workshop

Verdict

Glowing, and just like: you

How it works and launch plan

The campaign pieces

  • A shade quiz that ends in a shareable “Autopsy Report” card for stories, with the customer’s own findings on it
  • Autopsy tables at pop-ups: a swatch station where the wrong shade is wiped off and the right one is revealed
  • A reel series, “Cause of death: compromise”, that ends on “Cause of life”
  • A toe-tag insert in every pack that reads “Found alive”

How I’d launch it

  • Invite the workshop women to post their reports first, since their feedback shaped the product
  • Let creators share reports with their real shade matches, no retouching
  • Keep one stamp and one tag on every cover so the series is easy to spot
